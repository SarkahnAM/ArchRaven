ARCHRAVEN LIBRARY

A smarter, indie-first mobile bookshelf.

The “ArchRaven Library,” a smarter, indie-first mobile bookshelf in the palm of your hands. The ArchRaven Library focuses on indie-first publication to give the unheard a voice and the unseen a place to be found.

No gatekeeping due to store dependency. By design, it is a discovery tool for readers. No more long searches just to give up and retreat to your comfort read again and again.

ArchRaven Library offers a clean, smart way to organize and enjoy your E-books from the authors of the world, unknown and known, all in one place. To never be forgotten — just like the ArchRaven.
